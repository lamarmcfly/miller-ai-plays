## Common pitfalls

- **Check the AI-use rules and strip patient details before you paste anything.** Schools, programs, and application services set their own policies, and rare diagnoses, places, or dates can identify a patient. If a policy is unclear, ask your dean's office or advisor first.
- **Don't ask it to "fix" your draft.** The moment it writes sentences for you, you have lost your voice and possibly broken a policy. Ask for feedback and questions, then rewrite every line yourself.
- **The AI is not an advisor.** It does not know current norms for your specialty or what a specific program wants. Have a specialty advisor or mentor read the final version.
