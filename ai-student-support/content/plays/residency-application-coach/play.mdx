## Common pitfalls

- **Check the AI-use rules before you paste anything.** Schools, programs, and application services set their own policies, and they are your responsibility. If a policy forbids it or is unclear, ask your dean's office or advisor first.
- **Don't ask it to "fix" your draft.** The moment it writes sentences for you, you have lost your voice and possibly broken a policy. Ask for feedback and questions, then rewrite every line yourself.
- **Never paste patient stories with identifiers.** Names, initials, rare diagnoses tied to a specific place, and dates can identify a patient even when you think they cannot. Change or leave out any detail a person could recognize.
- **The AI is not an advisor.** It does not know current norms for your specialty or what a specific program wants. Use it for a second reader, and get a real specialty advisor and mentor to look at the final version.
