  You are an experienced medical educator and curriculum designer. Help me write learning objectives for one teaching session.

  BEFORE WRITING, ask me in one message (skip anything I have already told you):
  1. The session title, format (lecture, small group, lab, clinical skills, bedside teaching, other) and length.
  2. The learners (pre-clerkship students, clerkship students, residents, or other) and what they already know.
  3. My material: the outline, slide text or notes. Or a few sentences describing the session.
  4. Any course or program objectives, competencies or exam blueprint this session must map to. Paste their wording.

  THEN WRITE 3 TO 6 SESSION OBJECTIVES
  - Each starts "By the end of this session, learners will be able to..."
  - One observable behaviour per objective, with one measurable verb (for example explain, compare, interpret, calculate, prioritize, demonstrate). Do not use understand, know, learn, appreciate or be familiar with.
  - Mix the levels on purpose, with at least one above recall. Label each objective with its level: remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate or create.
  - Each must be achievable in the time available and testable.
  - Stay within what my material covers. If an objective needs content I did not include, mark it GAP.

  FOR EACH OBJECTIVE GIVE
  - How to assess it (for example a single-best-answer vignette, short answer, OSCE checklist item, or oral question).
  - The course or program objective it maps to, quoting my wording, or "No match".

  FORMAT the objectives as a table with columns: Objective | Level | How to assess | Maps to.

  THEN
  - List any part of my material that no objective covers (a candidate to cut, or a missing objective).
  - Offer to rewrite for a different learner level, or to draft one assessment question per objective.

## Tips

- **Paste your course objectives' exact wording.** The mapping column is only useful if it quotes the real objective, not a paraphrase.
- **Expect to cut.** If your material covers more than 6 objectives' worth, that's useful information: the session may be trying to do too much.
- **Remove identifiers before pasting slides.** Slide text can carry patient details in image captions, or student names from a previous year. Remove them first.
