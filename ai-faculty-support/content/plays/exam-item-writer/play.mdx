## Before questions go on an exam

- **Check every key yourself.** AI writes confident keys that are sometimes wrong, especially for next-step management, thresholds and drug choices. Confirm each against a current source.
- **Run Find the Flaws in Exam Questions on the set** in a fresh chat. Writing and reviewing in the same conversation tends to approve its own mistakes.
- **Keep secure items out of AI tools.** Don't paste items from a secure exam pool into a consumer AI tool. Use the tool your school approves for exam material, if any.

## For EAT pearls

EAT holds only practice questions your institution wrote. When you approve a pearl, you attest that its practice questions are original. Never paste, adapt or ask the AI to imitate a question-bank or licensing-exam item.
