## What this does not replace

The pre-check is a second pair of eyes, not the approval. When you press **Approve** in EAT you attest that **you** answered these questions for the pearl: that you opened each source, checked the claim against it, and thought through the exceptions. The AI cannot open your sources and can be wrong about the medicine. Treat every PASS as "nothing obvious," not as "verified."

## Tips

- **Paste the source passages.** With them, the AI can quote the words that do or don't support the takeaway, which is the check most often skipped.
- **Pre-check a pearl in a fresh chat.** If you drafted it in the same conversation, the AI tends to approve its own work.
- **Learner reports.** When EAT shows reports against a pearl, paste the pearl and the report reason (inaccurate, outdated, unclear, not relevant). Never paste anything about the learner who reported it; EAT doesn't show you who that was, by design.
